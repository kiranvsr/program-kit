# Freeze Review

*Stage 5 artifact. An independent reviewer, never the author, checks stages 1 to 4 together. Each lens records findings and its own sign-off.*

## Architect

*Findings on structure, boundaries, technology choices and the fit between the specification and the architecture. Each finding names the file, the problem and the fix.*

Sign-off: name, date, and whether every finding is closed.

## Engineering

*Findings on buildability, testability, size of the work, and the evidence the gates can really produce.*

Sign-off: name, date, and whether every finding is closed.

## Program

*Findings on scope, sequencing, dependencies, and whether every journey and requirement is covered and owned.*

Sign-off: name, date, and whether every finding is closed.

## Project

*Findings on plan, capacity, risks, assumptions and open questions that still lack an owner or a date.*

Sign-off: name, date, and whether every finding is closed.

## Delivery

*Findings on environments, release and rollback, operations, and every claim that needs an environment that does not exist.*

Sign-off: name, date, and whether every finding is closed.
