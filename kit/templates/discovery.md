# Discovery

*Stage 1 artifact. Facts and decisions from the discovery interview; every statement names its source in the ledger.*

## Purpose

*What the program is for, in a few sentences, and how success is judged.*

## Users And Stakeholders

*Who uses it, who pays for it, who approves it, and who is affected without using it.*

## Existing Landscape

*Systems, data, code, processes and contracts that exist today and that this program must work with or replace.*

## Scope Edges

*What is explicitly in, what is explicitly out, and what is deliberately left for later.*

## Constraints

*Dates, budget, mandated and forbidden technology, organisational rules, and anything else that limits the choices.*

## Environments

*Every environment the program will touch, and whether it exists today. The matrix itself lives in `environments.yaml`; summarise it here.*

## Team And Ways Of Working

*Who builds and reviews, their roles and availability, the branching and release practice, and how decisions are made.*

## Risks And Unknowns

*What could derail the program and what is not yet known, each with an owner and a date by which it must be settled.*

## Regulatory

*Laws, standards, certifications, audits and data-handling obligations that apply, or a statement that none do and who confirmed it.*
