# Specification

*Stage 3 artifact. Frozen at approval 5; after that it changes only through change requests.*

## Requirements

*One entry per requirement with an ID (REQ-001, ...), each traced to at least one journey or to a named cross-cutting concern, and each testable.*

## Non-Functional Requirements

*One entry per requirement with an ID (NFR-001, ...) covering scale, availability, latency, security, compliance, tenancy, data retention and residency. Each has a measurable target, or the words "no target yet" with an owner and a date.*

## Domain Objects And Interfaces

*The objects the system manages, their fields and lifecycles, and every interface it offers or consumes with its contract.*

## Security And Tenancy Boundaries

*Trust boundaries, who may do what, how tenants are kept apart, how secrets and personal data are handled.*

## Failure Modes And Recovery

*For each way the system can fail: how it is detected, what the user sees, how it recovers, and what data is at risk.*

## Observability

*The logs, metrics, traces and alerts needed to run the system, and who acts on them.*

## Open Questions

*Each question with an owner and a date. A question is closed by an answer or a ruling, and the entry then points to it.*

## Change Log

*Date, change request or ruling, what changed, and the IDs it touches. Empty until the first change after approval.*
